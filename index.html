About Dilo Perdi

Dilo perdi (“lost water”) is a volunteer project with no money behind it and no link to the CWA or the government.

Anyone can add a leak they see on a street or in a public place. Each report is checked by hand before it appears. The map shows how many days each leak has been there, so everyone can see what is still waiting for repair.

Your privacy. We do not ask for your name, phone or email. Your photo is reduced in size before it is sent. Photos showing people or car plates are removed.

The map does not send reports to the CWA.
